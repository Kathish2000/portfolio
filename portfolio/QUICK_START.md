## ⚡ Quick Setup (5 Minutes)

1. **Open `index.html`** in your browser to see the site

2. **Replace Placeholders** - Use Find & Replace in your editor:
   - `[Your Name]` → Your actual name
   - `[Job Title]` → Your job title
   - `[Industry]` → Your industry
   - `[Your Email]` → Your email
   - `[LinkedIn URL]` → Your LinkedIn
   - `[GitHub URL]` → Your GitHub

3. **Customize Content**:
   - Add your projects in `projects.html`
   - Update experience in `experience.html`
   - Add skills in `skills.html`
   - Update resume in `resume.html`

4. **Test & Deploy**:
   - Test all pages
   - Generate PDF resume (Print → Save as PDF)
   - Deploy to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your hosting

## 🎨 Customization Tips

### Change Colors
Edit `css/style.css` - Look for `:root` variables at the top

### Add Images
Replace placeholder divs with `<img>` tags:
```html
<img src="images/project1.jpg" alt="Project Name">
```

### Add More Projects
Copy a project card in `projects.html` and customize it

### Customize Resume
The resume in `resume.html` is print-ready. Use browser's Print function to save as PDF.
